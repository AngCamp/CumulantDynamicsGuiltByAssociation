# Grassmanian Tests

The grassmanian in very simple terms is a manifold on which a family of PCA analyses can live.  This is an attempt to try to use this method to work neural analysis.
We will be studying it in the Dandisets 000363 and 001695, ALM in delayed decision making task, and the CA1 in spatial nav, sleep and odor tasks.  Both datasets have other regions but this is the start.

This might be worth reading: 
Bendokat, T., Zimmermann, R., & Absil, P. A. (2024). A Grassmann manifold handbook: basic geometry and computational aspects: T. Bendokat et al. Advances in Computational Mathematics, 50(1), 6.
